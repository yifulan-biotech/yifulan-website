Evidence

The document most suppliers cannot send

Ask any supplier for their test data and watch what happens. Most will send you a raw material specification sheet — the ingredient was tested, so the product is assumed to work.

Ours is measured on the finished formula, on human subjects, over 28 days, by an accredited third-party laboratory. The protocol records measurements at baseline, ten minutes, day 7 and day 28.

We always give you three things with any number we quote: which part of the body or face was measured, over how many days, and by what percentage. A number without those three things is not evidence — it is decoration.
