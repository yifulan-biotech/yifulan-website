What is a Micro-Energy Body?

It is not about how much you put in. It is about how precisely it gets there.

Most skincare brands can list ingredients. Far fewer can explain how an ingredient reaches the cell that needs it — or show a measurement for the result. This page is about the second part.

Where it sits

Skincare has moved in three steps.

1.0

Basic protection

Cleanse, moisturise, block the sun. Skin is treated as a surface that has to be covered and kept clean.

2.0

Repair and replenish

Identify what the skin appears to lack and put it back. Effective — but one formula is sold to everyone, regardless of what their skin actually needs.

3.0

Precision delivery

Keep the active, change how it is delivered — to the cells that need it, rather than across the whole face. This is the step the Micro-Energy Body belongs to.

The platform

A delivery platform, not an ingredient.

The Micro-Energy Body is a carrier, not an active. It is what the rest of the formula is built on.

50 nm

Carrier size

Each carrier is 50 nanometres across — small enough to pass through the outer layers rather than sit on top of them.

Bionic

Recognition sites

The carrier surface carries a bionic recognition site, designed to dock with the receptor cell it is meant for. A key, not a broadcast.

6

Formulation patents

Six core formulation patents are filed around this platform. They are searchable — ask us for the numbers.

The platform carries eight technology streams, including encapsulation for trans-membrane delivery, fibroblast activation, mitochondrial energy regulation, cellular immune regulation and a five-channel approach to tone. Products are manufactured in China to GMP standards.

What this page is not
