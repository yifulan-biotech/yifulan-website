Technology

What is WEINENGTI?

It is not about how much you put in. It is about how precisely it gets there.

WEINENGTI is a vesicle — tens of nanometres across — that carries a group of active substances inside one shell. Not a single molecule, and not a plant extract: several actives, held together in one carrier, delivered together.

It is best understood as one ingredient, and a hard one to make. The process that produces it is protected by patent.

Composition

A carrier is only worth as much as what is inside it.

Four active substances travel in the vesicle. The shell does the protecting; the surface does the finding.

01 · Adenosine

A nucleoside that sits in the cell's energy metabolism. In the routine it is the part that supports how the cell handles its own energy.

02 · Sodium DNA

Supplies the raw material a cell draws on when it rebuilds its own structures. Our internal name for it is blunt: the repair material.

03 · Signal peptides

The signalling half of the formula — short peptides that carry the message the rest of the material is there to deliver.

04 · NAD⁺

Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide. A coenzyme at the centre of cellular energy metabolism, involved in a long list of enzyme reactions.

These are cosmetic ingredients, described here at the level of what they are. What they do on skin is measured by report, not by adjective — see the evidence section below.

Production

One patented step does the work.

The actives are produced in a controlled cell-culture process. They are then passed through a proprietary extrusion step — the patent at the centre of this platform — which forms the vesicle and loads it in the same pass.

01

Source material

Actives produced under controlled culture conditions, to a fixed specification, batch after batch.

02

Patented extrusion

A proprietary extrusion process forms the carrier and encloses the actives. This is the step the patent protects.

03

Loaded vesicle

Out comes WEINENGTI — one material, four actives, a recognition surface already in place.

Six core formulation patents are filed around this platform, and they are searchable. Ask us for the patent numbers with your enquiry.

Mechanism

Targeting first, then a slow release.

The usual problem is not a shortage of active. It is that most of it ends up in the wrong place — spread across the whole face, or sitting on the surface. The platform changes the delivery, not the ingredient.

Where it sits

Skincare has moved in three steps.

1.0

Basic protection

Cleanse, moisturise, block the sun. Skin is treated as a surface that has to be covered and kept clean.

2.0

Repair and replenish

Identify what the skin appears to lack and put it back. Effective — but one formula is sold to everyone, regardless of what their skin actually needs.

3.0

Precision delivery

Keep the active, change how it is delivered — to the cells that need it, rather than across the whole face. This is the step WEINENGTI belongs to.

Eight technology streams sit on the platform: encapsulation for trans-membrane delivery, fibroblast activation, mitochondrial energy regulation, cellular immune regulation, a five-channel approach to tone, and three more. Products are made by our contract manufacturing partner — a GMP-certified facility — to our specification.
