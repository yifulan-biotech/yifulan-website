About Yifulan

Cell biotechnology, applied to skincare.

Chongqing Yifulan Biotechnology Co., Ltd. is a cell biotechnology company based in Chongqing, China — and the company that brings WEINENGTI skincare to overseas markets.

01 · What we do

Cell technology, then skin

Our work is cell technology and its application to skin. The WEINENGTI platform — a 50 nm vesicle carrier with bionic recognition sites — is developed and supplied by us. Every product in the WEINENGTI range is built on it, and six core formulation patents are filed around it.

02 · How it is made

We own the formula, not a factory

Production is carried out by our contract manufacturing partner — a GMP-certified facility — to our specification. That is deliberate: the technology, the raw material and the formulation stay under our control, and every product can be traced back to a formula and a test report.

03 · Where we are

Chongqing, Liangjiang New Area

We are based in the Dadi International Biomedicine Industrial Park, in Chongqing's Liangjiang New Area — a life-science cluster that the municipal government and the district administration support and have brought in as a priority industry.
