What we do

A cell biotechnology company,
with a contract manufacturer behind it.

Yifulan is a cell biotechnology company based in Chongqing. We develop the WEINENGTI platform, supply the raw material and set the formulation. Production is carried out by our contract manufacturing partner, a GMP-certified facility, built to our specification. Everything that sits between your purchase order and your warehouse is our job.

What we own, and what we do not. The platform technology, the raw material and the formulation are ours. We do not own a factory — production is run by our contract manufacturing partner under our specification. That is why every product can be traced back to a formula and a test report, rather than to a listing we picked up from someone else.
